SASS evidence from the B300-built official FlashKDA extension
===============================================================

Binary:
  assignment02/FlashKDA/flash_kda_C.cpython-312-x86_64-linux-gnu.so

Command:
  cuobjdump --dump-sass flash_kda_C.cpython-312-x86_64-linux-gnu.so

Counts in the dumped SASS:
  HMMA.16816.F32.BF16   1520
  HMMA.16816.F16          24
  UTMALDG.3D            1086
  UTCMMA / UTCHMMA / TCGEN05 / WGMMA   no matching lines in official extension

Representative instructions include HMMA.16816.F32.BF16 in the recurrence
path and UTMALDG.3D for TMA global-to-shared loads.  Thus the extension does
use asynchronous TMA movement, but its matrix multiplication remains the
SM80-style HMMA path; it does not contain a tcgen05 UMMA instruction.

As a cross-check, the separate tcgen05 microbenchmark's SASS contains 10
UTCHMMA instructions and 16 LDTM instructions.  That confirms the toolchain
and architecture can emit the tcgen05/TMEM path; its absence in the official
extension is a real code-generation/design choice rather than a B300 decoder
limitation.
