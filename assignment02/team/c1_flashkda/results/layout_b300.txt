CUTLASS TMEM layout evidence (layout_dump)
==========================================

64x16 FP32 accumulator layout:
  tmem layout=(((_16,_4),_16),_1,_1):(((_65536,_2097152),_1),_0,_0)
  coalesced=(_16,_4,_16):(_65536,_2097152,_1)

Tmem copy upcast layouts:
  SM100_TMEM_LOAD_32dp32b1x: upcast=(_1,_32):(_1,_65536)
  SM100_TMEM_LOAD_16dp32b1x: upcast=(_2,_16):(_1,_65536)

The 16dp32b1x form matches the 64x16 accumulator fragment and compiles/runs
correctly in the microbenchmark; the default 32dp32b1x form does not match
this fragment.  This is a concrete example of why switching to UMMA also
requires a TMEM layout/epilogue redesign, not just replacing `gemm()`.
